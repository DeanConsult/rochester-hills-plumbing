What We Do

Plumbing Services in Rochester Hills MI

Rochester Hills Plumbing LLC is your full-service plumbing company for both residential and commercial repair and installation. If you don’t see what you need, just call us.

Drain Cleaning

Clogged drains, sinks, toilets, and floor drains cleared fast. We have the tools and experience to get your drain repaired safely and quickly — whether the cause is grease, debris, hair, or tree roots.

Pipe Leak & Crack Repair

A pipe leak can cause major damage fast. Shut off your water first, then call us. We repair and replace all types of pipes quickly and get your water service back online with minimal disruption.

Frozen Pipe Repair

Michigan winters mean frozen pipes — and we’ve been fixing them in Rochester Hills for over 20 years. We respond fast to burst pipes, and can also advise you on preventing freezes before they happen.

Water Heater Repair & Replacement

We service all makes and models of water heaters. We carry parts on our trucks so most repairs are done in a single visit. Installations come with a warranty from both the manufacturer and from us.

Faucet, Fixture & Disposal

Leaky faucets, shower and tub repair, garbage disposal repair and replacement, instant-hot installation, refrigerator water lines, and more. We work with Delta, Moen, Kohler, and American Standard.

Emergency Plumbing

We understand that plumbing problems don’t wait for business hours. Rochester Hills Plumbing LLC offers 24-hour emergency service. Call us anytime and we’ll get a plumber to you as fast as possible.
